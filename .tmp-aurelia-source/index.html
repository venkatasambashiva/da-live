Common questions

Quick answers before you talk to sales

Can Helix Cloud connect to instruments we already own?
Yes — Helix Cloud ships with connector modules for most Aurelia instruments back to 2016, plus a generic CSV/API bridge for third-party systems.
Do you offer financing or leasing?
Most instrument purchases can be structured as a lease or grant-cycle-aligned payment plan — ask your account manager for a quote alongside the standard price.
How long does installation and qualification take?
Typical IQ/OQ for a single LX-9000 or MX-410 takes 2–3 business days once the instrument is on-site, performed by an Aurelia field engineer.
